About

The agency

Founded in 2026, Nova Talent Management is a South African talent agency dedicated to discovering, developing and representing fresh dynamic talent across the acting and modelling industry

We believe great talent can come from anywhere. Our mission is to uncover distinctive new faces and personalities, provide them with meaningful opportunities, and connect them with brands, productions and creative teams that value authentic talent With a focus on commercial work, film, television, advertising and modelling, Nova Talent Management takes a fresh and forward-thinking approach to talent representation. We are passionate about building careers, not just casting faces, supporting our talent as they grow, evolve and make their mark in the industry

Ethical management

Transparent contracts, fair pay, and models who are informed partners in every booking decision.

Creative diversity

We build rosters that reflect the industries we work with, not a single narrow standard.
